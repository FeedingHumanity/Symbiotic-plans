**Moon Base Technical Design**  
**MoonBaseAlpha v8.6C-TD**  
**Location: Shackleton Crater Rim**  
**October 2026**

### 1. Objectives
- Permanent, self-expanding, radiation-shielded lunar outpost.
- Continuous production of protected subsurface volume as the foundation for all functions.
- High-cadence manufacture and electromagnetic export of large orbital compute nodes (StarMind AI1 class).
- Closed mass, energy, and thermal loops using local resources.
- Support for multi-generational human presence.

### 2. Master Constraint
Shielded volume growth rate from continuous tunneling is the single rate-limiter. Every other capability scales with it.

### 3. Physical Foundation – Moonworm Tunnel System
Modified Prufrock-family TBM adapted for vacuum, abrasive regolith, 1/6 g, and real-time ISRU lining (sintered/extruded regolith).

Primary outputs:
- Radiation-shielded habitats and fabrication bays
- Protected corridors for thermal arteries and railgun track
- Spoil for lining and progressive soil genesis

Target: continuous advance, >90 % ISRU lining, full robotic autonomy.

### 4. Thermal Architecture – Tri-Web
Three independent geographic arteries, each sized for 50 % of peak load. Loss of any one still yields full capacity. Cross-ties every 200 m with fast isolation. Localized PCM (lithium nitrate trihydrate) provides ≥72 h survival under total disconnection.

Priority: habitat → ISRU → industrial loads → electrical efficiency → radiative rejection.

### 5. Power
**Bootstrap:** 6 repurposed Raptors on expended Starship hulls → multi-MW electric via turbogenerators.  
**Steady-state:** Shackleton-rim solar + storage + nuclear SMRs + waste-heat recovery.  
Non-critical loads draw only from surplus after survival, production, and railgun priorities.

### 6. Closed-Loop Architecture
- **Orbital debris:** Refined in LEO; only high-value items transfer lunar-ward.
- **Food/biomass:** Produced inside tunnels; progressive soil from regolith + biochar + microbes.
- **Waste:** Near-zero waste, >95 % water recovery, pyrolysis to biochar/syngas.
- **Fabrication:** Shielded industrial volume for structural/thermal elements of AI1 nodes. Target >50 % lunar-origin mass fraction at maturity.

### 7. Electromagnetic Export
Magnetic railgun using surplus power. Exit velocity ≈ 2.38 km/s. Track corridors excavated and protected by Moonworm. Eliminates propellant mass for high-cadence structural export.

### 8. AI1 Node Baseline
20–30 m height, 70–75 m wingspan, 150–210+ kW solar, high-performance compute, 110–160 m² radiators. Lunar contribution focused on high-mass structural and thermal elements.

### 9. Critical Path
1. Earth vacuum + simulant validation of full Moonworm (cutter + continuous lining + dust control).
2. Stationary Raptor-to-electric power demonstration.
3. Uncrewed lunar delivery and continuous volume creation.
4. Thermal network → fabrication volume → progressive localization → railgun corridors.

**Core principle:** Volume first. Everything else scales with it.
